🚀 INSTANT DEPLOYMENT GUIDE - Get Your Live Link in 2 Minutes!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CHOOSE YOUR PLATFORM:

OPTION A: Vercel (Easiest - Recommended)
───────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Click "Add New" → "Project"
3. Select "LakshmiRocky1998/Crochet-Bussiness" repo
4. Click "Deploy"
5. Wait ~1-2 minutes... DONE! ✅

Your live URL will be:
https://crochet-bussiness.vercel.app

(Vercel auto-deploys whenever you push to GitHub!)


OPTION B: Netlify Drop (Absolute Easiest - Instant!)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to https://netlify.com/drop
2. Find the "public" folder in your repo
3. Drag & drop the "public" folder
4. INSTANT live link! 🎉

Your live URL will be:
https://[random-name].netlify.app


OPTION C: Netlify with GitHub (Recommended)
──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to https://netlify.com
2. Click "Add new site" → "Import an existing project"
3. Choose "GitHub"
4. Select "LakshmiRocky1998/Crochet-Bussiness"
5. Set:
   - Build command: (leave empty)
   - Publish directory: public
6. Click "Deploy"
7. Done! ✅

Your live URL will be:
https://crochet-bussiness.netlify.app
